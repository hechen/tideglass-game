Serve the game locally with `python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory game`, then open http://localhost:8000 in a browser.

## Night Tow

Choose **Night Tow**, then **Launch rescue**. Steer with arrows or WASD, hold on the sea to
steer toward a point, or hold the on-screen arrows. When the cutter is inside the dotted
ring and pointing generally toward the lifeboat, press Space or **Throw line**. The same
control releases the line. Tow the lifeboat into the gold harbor ring. Avoid the surf rings
around reefs; broken hull or tow, or an expired clock, ends the run. Rough water deflects
the boats. P or **Pause** pauses; **Restart** immediately restarts the run. Sound is off
until enabled. The long line shortens automatically inside the harbor, drawing the trailing
lifeboat in when the cutter stops near the center. Reduced-motion preference makes the beam
and water still and suppresses the moving wake.

Three passages award 100 points per life, 150 for a tow without a reef strike or snapped
line, and two points per whole second left. Total possible lives: nine. The next passage
button continues the run, and the final report offers immediate replay.

The canvas uses a 1000 × 600 world. Coordinates below are approximate targets, not
automated inputs; hold on each point or steer toward it. Give the cutter time to turn and
keep the rope clear of reefs on the return:

1. **Lantern Reach:** start (240, 390); head east toward (590, 390), then the lifeboat
   near (680, 390). Throw the line from inside its dotted ring. Return toward (590, 390),
   then the harbor near (150, 390).
2. **The Black Narrows:** start (230, 395); travel via (400, 235) and (690, 210) to the
   lifeboat near (760, 270). Attach, then return via (690, 210), (400, 235), and the
   harbor near (145, 395). The surf-ringed reef near (490, 365) blocks the direct route.
3. **Stormglass Point:** start (230, 405); travel via (380, 210) and (700, 220) to the
   lifeboat near (790, 350). Attach and reverse those waypoints to the harbor near
   (145, 405). Keep the trailing boat clear of both reefs.

Selectors for ordinary browser play: `[data-action="night-tow"]`, `[data-tow="launch"]`,
`#night-tow-canvas`, `[data-tow="line"]`, `[data-tow="pause"]`,
`[data-tow="next"]`, and `[data-tow="restart"]`. Direction buttons use
`[data-steer="up"]`, `down`, `left`, and `right`. The dotted ring and orange flare
provide a useful capture just before attachment; the curved gold line, cutter and
lifeboat are visible during a normal return voyage.

## Other modes

**Stormwatch Dispatch** is the turn-based rescue shift. The four chart buttons start the
original lantern campaign; later charts unlock as earlier charts are completed. Their
keyboard and pointer controls remain on screen. Best scores are stored locally when the
browser permits it.

## Packaging inventory

`game/` contains the offline HTML, CSS, game controllers, original vector/canvas art,
simulation, chart data, and rules. `main.ts` checks these assets and three or four real
screenshots, clears generated output assets, copies `game/` and `screenshots/`, and uses
the unchanged `vendor/makefun-game.ts` to build the dossier. With Node 22 or newer:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/path/to/clean-output node main.ts
```

Screenshots are captured separately through normal gameplay and are not generated by the
game or this build script. The repository is MIT licensed.
